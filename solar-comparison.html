The Real Cost
Mortgage Offset — The Hidden Factor

The $19,460 comes from the BankSA mortgage offset account (balance ~$104,711, variable rate 6.04%). Money sitting in offset effectively earns 6.04% tax-free by reducing mortgage interest. Taking it out means paying more interest — this is a real cost that simple payback calculations ignore.

But here's the key: solar savings flow back into the offset account, gradually rebuilding the balance. Each year the offset deficit shrinks, so the interest penalty decreases. The system eventually repays itself in full.
