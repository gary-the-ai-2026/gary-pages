Risk Alert
The Glow Inverter Bottleneck

GoodWe's 9.99kW inverter is the single biggest risk in the Glow quote:

  • Winter already hits 5–9 kW peak draw without pool, AC, or sauna — that's 50–90% of capacity on a mild day.
  • Summer pool pump (+1.5–2 kW) + AC (+3–5 kW) + sauna (+3–6 kW) + baseline = 8.5–15 kW total.
  • If load exceeds 9.99 kW, the inverter can't supply all of it — you import from grid at peak prices even with a full battery.
  • At 50c/kWh, importing 2 kW for 4 hours on 30 summer days = $120/yr in avoidable grid costs — conservative.
  • Worst case: 38°C February day. Pool + AC + sauna + oven = 14 kW draw. GoodWe clips at 9.99 kW. That one day costs $8–12 NRG avoids entirely.

Over 10 summers, the inverter bottleneck alone costs $1,500–2,500 in unnecessary grid imports — invisible in the initial quote.

  3. Dual-glass panels for a forever home. Trina's bifacial dual-glass panels have better humidity/fire resistance and lower degradation than Jinko's standard backsheet.
  4. Future-proofed. Built-in EV charger integration, blackout protection, and modular battery expansion. You're not locked into today's config.
