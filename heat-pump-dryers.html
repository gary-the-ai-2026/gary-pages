What the retailer charts won't tell you

  • The #1 best-seller on Appliances Online (Bosch Series 8 WQG24201AU, $2,049) has a 2.7/5 consumer rating from 293 reviews on ProductReview.com.au. Best-selling ≠ best.
  • The #2 best-seller (Haier HDHPS80KW1, $999) scores 2.8/5 from owners. Great price, polarising reliability.
  • CHOICE recommends heat pump dryers starting around $1,500 — models below that involve reliability trade-offs.
  • Cycle times are the #1 Reddit complaint across all brands. Expect 2–3 hours for a full load. Bosch is the fastest; everyone else trails.
  • F&P DH8060 has a 2.1/5 on ProductReview (15 reviews) for a specific variant (DH8060P3) — verify which sub-model you're looking at.
