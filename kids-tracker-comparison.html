Context & Caveats
The honest trade-offs
The Ace 3's $66.98 is a genuine deal — but it's clearing for a reason. Google discontinued it as it consolidates the kids' line into the Ace LTE. Parent forums report worsening setup and sync glitches, and long-term app support isn't guaranteed.
Neither watch has GPS or calling. Both are pure activity + sleep trackers. If location tracking becomes important (e.g. for school excursions), you'd step up to the Garmin Bounce (~2× the price).
The Garmin's 1-year battery is the decisive practical win. A 6-year-old will not remember to charge a watch every week. With the Garmin you set it up and forget about charging for a year — and the chores-and-rewards system is genuinely motivating for kids.
