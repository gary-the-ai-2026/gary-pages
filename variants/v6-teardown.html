The Audit

Step by step: where leads go cold.

Six stages. Each one is a checkpoint where the prospect either moves forward — or disappears. Let's examine each.

Step 1 of 6

Lead arrives on your website

Manual Bottleneck
Prospect fills in contact form → Data lands in your inbox → Sits there until you check email → You read it, assess it manually → You decide whether to follow up → You reply or call

Observed: This is the first loss point. If you're on a call or it's after hours, the lead waits. Every hour of delay reduces close probability by 7–10%. A lead arriving at 9pm waits until the next morning. By then, a competitor may have already responded.

→ AI Fix
Sales Agent monitors the inbox 24/7. Within minutes of form submission, it qualifies the lead (industry, team size, quote volume, urgency signals), sends a personalised acknowledgment, and preps a summary for your review. You see it when you're ready — but the lead doesn't wait.
Step 2 of 6

Lead qualification and scoring

No System
You read the email → You gauge whether this is a "good" lead (gut feel) → You prioritise based on recency, not value → High-value leads get same treatment as tyre-kickers → No scoring, no tiering, no urgency flag

Observed: Without a scoring system, every lead gets equal attention. But a 15-quote-per-month commercial contractor is worth 5x more than a one-off residential inquiry. When both land in the same inbox, the one you reply to first is whichever you saw first — not the one worth more.

→ AI Fix
Sales Agent scores every lead on a 0–100 scale using: monthly quote volume, average job value, industry segment, urgency keywords, and whether they mention a current provider. High-value leads get priority alerts. Low-value leads get automated nurturing. You focus on what matters.
Step 3 of 6

Information gathering and prep

Time Drain
You need details from the prospect → You email/call asking for them → Wait 1–3 days for a response → You chase → They reply partially → You ask again → Loop continues → Days turn into a week → Momentum is dead

Observed: Back-and-forth information gathering is the single biggest killer of deal momentum. Each exchange adds 24–72 hours. Prospects lose interest. The "exciting new opportunity" becomes a chore.

→ AI Fix
Sales Agent sends a structured intake form on first contact — short, specific, pre-filled where possible. Follows up automatically at 24h and 48h if incomplete. When the form is done, it compiles a briefing packet: prospect profile, estimated deal value, suggested approach, and two time slots for your call.
Step 4 of 6

Scheduling the sales call

Calendar Chaos
You propose a time → They can't make it → You propose another → They're busy → 3–4 emails later, you find a slot → By now it's next week → The lead has cooled → You show up, they cancel or no-show

Observed: Scheduling friction kills more deals than bad pitches. Every "does Thursday work? No? How about Friday?" email erodes the prospect's perception of your professionalism.

→ AI Fix
Agent surfaces your available slots (calibrated to your calendar), sends exactly two options, and books the confirmed time instantly. Sends calendar invite with prep notes. Sends a reminder 2 hours before. The scheduling loop collapses from days to minutes.
Step 5 of 6

Post-call follow-up and quoting

Forgetfulness
Great call → You promise to send a quote → You write the quote (manual) → You send it → Then… silence → You forget to follow up at day 3 → Lead goes cold → Two weeks later you remember → Too late

Observed: The post-call follow-up sequence is where most solo operators lose the deal. They're already onto the next lead. The follow-up cadence — day 1, day 3, day 7, day 14 — requires a system. Memory alone doesn't cut it.

→ AI Fix
Sales Agent triggers a follow-up sequence automatically after the call: sends a thank-you and quote summary (day 1), a value-add follow-up (day 3), a "checking in, any questions?" (day 7), and a final nudge (day 14). Each message is personalised with call notes. You approve before send.
Step 6 of 6

Post-close: invoicing, reviews, retention

Neglected
Deal closed → You do the work → Weeks later you remember to invoice → You send it → You forget to chase payment → You forget to ask for a review → Client fades into the archive → No referral, no review, no repeat

Observed: The hidden cost: a satisfied client who never leaves a review, never refers, and never re-engages. Not because they wouldn't — because nobody asked them. Every closed deal is a missed opportunity for social proof and repeat business.

→ AI Fix
Finance Agent sends the invoice on closing day. Tracks payment, sends reminders at day 7 and day 14. Customer Service Agent follows up 2 days after completion: requests a review, checks satisfaction, and flags any issues. Analytics Agent tracks repeat potential and flags re-engagement opportunities.
