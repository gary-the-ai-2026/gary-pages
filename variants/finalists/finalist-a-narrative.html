The Questions

The questions you've been asking.

Walk through the Before/After and certain questions come up naturally. Let's address them directly.

Question 1
"What happens to my leads at 2am?"
Right now? They sit in your inbox. A tradie fills in your form at 11pm after a long day on the tools. By 2am, they've had a reply from a competitor who uses automated acknowledgment. By 8am, when you open your laptop, the lead has already formed a first impression — of someone who responded faster than you.

With AI: Your sales agent doesn't sleep. When that 2am lead comes in, it's acknowledged within minutes — not with a generic auto-reply, but with a personalised message that qualifies their needs and suggests next steps. The lead wakes up to a response. You wake up to a qualified lead summary.
Question 2
"What about the leads that go cold?"
They're not bad leads. They're leads that weren't nurtured. A three-day gap in communication drops close probability by 30% or more. By day seven, the lead has mentally moved on — not because they chose someone else, but because the momentum died.

With AI: Your sales agent runs a structured follow-up cadence: day 1 thank-you, day 3 value-add, day 7 check-in, day 14 final nudge. Each message is personalised based on the original conversation. You approve before send. No lead goes cold because nobody remembered to follow up.
Question 3
"Is AI actually ready for a real business?"
This is the one that stops most people. The fear that AI is a toy — fine for generating images or writing emails, but not trustworthy enough for real business operations. It's a fair question. And the answer depends entirely on how it's set up.

With AI: This isn't a chatbot you open in a browser. It's a team of specialist agents running on production infrastructure — the same agent framework (Hermes) and language model (DeepSeek) that have been running 24/7 in production for 4+ months across 18 platforms. It operates with a human-in-the-loop approval model: agents act, you review, agents learn. Mistakes happen once. The system gets smarter every week.
