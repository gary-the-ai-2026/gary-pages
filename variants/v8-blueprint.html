Design Principles

How the system is built.

Good architecture follows principles. These are the rules that govern how the system operates — and why it's reliable, not brittle.

🔁
Principle 1

Closed Loop

Every agent action flows through: Agent Acts → Human Reviews → Human Approves → Agent Learns. Mistakes happen once. The system compounds in intelligence.

🔒
Principle 2

Human in the Loop

Agents can draft, suggest, and prepare. They cannot execute irreversible actions without approval. The boundary is clear: agents propose, humans confirm.

📡
Principle 3

Your Tools, Your Rules

The system integrates with tools you already use — email, WhatsApp, Asana. Zero new apps. Zero new logins. The agents report to you where you already work.
