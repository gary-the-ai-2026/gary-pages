Need to bail early? Book the full day for the inclusions but ask at check-in about taking an earlier return. The half-day boats depart Green Island at ~12:00pm and ~4:00pm. Crew will usually let you hop on if there's space — especially with young kids. Not guaranteed on paper, but rarely an issue in practice.
