Amber vs Origin SSO

Same actual usage, two retailers — built from the real Amber bill (133.41 kWh imported · 114.94 kWh exported).

Our Pick

Stay on Amber

$86.76 /mo v $105–114
  1. Network supply charges are identical — $1.0526/day (network + metering) is an SA Power Networks pass-through every retailer bills unchanged.
  2. The real gap is feed-in: Amber pays wholesale 11.12c/kWh; Origin SSO pays a fixed 2c — a $10.48/month loss on the same 115 kWh.
  3. Origin’s retail fee ($23.19 baked into supply) ≈ Amber’s subscription ($23.16) — a wash.
  4. Origin’s free 12–3pm window can’t rescue it here: at 58.84c peak TOU its usage cost overshoots, and the 2c feed-in dwarfs the benefit.

Net 31-day cost: Amber $86.76 · Origin SSO ~$105–114 (2c FiT) / ~$98–107 (8c Solar Boost). Even the better Origin variant loses.
