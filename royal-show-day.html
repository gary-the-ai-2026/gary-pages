Our loop around the grounds

The station sits on the western edge; the main gate is on Goodwood Road to the east. We'll loop anti-clockwise, showbags first while lines are short, then farm, woodchop, food and rides on the way back out. Leave by ~2:15–2:30 for the 2:38 train.
