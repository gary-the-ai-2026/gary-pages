On the ground
Walking route

The station sits right against the showgrounds on the Goodwood Road side — no shuttle needed.

  • Adelaide Showground Station
    Get off and follow the footbridge / path towards Goodwood Road.
  • Main gates — Goodwood Road
    Two minutes' walk. Tap in, scan tickets, pick up a map.
  • Showbags first (left)
    Grab the bags early when queues are shortest.
  • Animals & Aggie's Farm (south)
    Ring, woodchop and farmyard — a to-do when the kids tire of walking.
  • Main Arena & food (centre)
    Lunch stop and arena ring, then rides on the way back out.
  • Back to the station (~2:15)
    Walk back the way we came — the exit is beside the entrance.
