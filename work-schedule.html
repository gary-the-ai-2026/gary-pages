The Logic
Why This Works
🔄

Same rhythm, every single week

Mon office, Tue WFH, Wed office, Thu–Fri off. No fortnightly switching, no mental load remembering which week is which.

🏃

Amy only does Rose Park once a fortnight

On her work Tuesday, it's a clean loop: Ashford → Toby (2:50) → Noah (3:15) → home. Every other day, the childcare run is Josh's or not needed.

⏱️

Toby gets one long day, one short day

Monday is the trade-off: ~8.4 hrs at childcare. Tuesday is ~6.8 hrs (or Donna's). Wed–Fri at home. No OSHC required.

🏡

Josh's WFH day lands midweek

Tuesday from home breaks up the commute, gives presence for the morning chaos, and means Toby's childcare day is short on Amy's work weeks.
