14-Day Route

Christchurch loop via the West Coast, Queenstown, Milford Sound & Aoraki. Tap the map markers or legend to explore each day.

Campsite Suggestions
DayCampsiteTypeWhy
1 Kowai Pass Domain, Springfield DOC Easy first night. Grassy domain by the Kowai River, basic but pleasant. 5 min from Castle Hill turnoff.
Klondyke Corner DOC Open valley at the junction of the Waimakariri & Bealey Rivers. 8 km from Arthur's Pass village. Big sky, mountain views.
2 Lake Pearson (Moana Rua) DOC Stillwater lake with mountain reflections. Halfway between Arthur's Pass and Hokitika. Quiet, sheltered.
Lake Mahinapua DOC 10 min south of Hokitika. Forest-fringed lake, good swimming. Popular but spacious.
Shining Star Beachfront, Hokitika Park Right on the beach. Watch the sunset over the Tasman. Good facilities, short walk to glowworm dell.
3 Lake Ianthe DOC Peaceful forest lake south of Hokitika. Mirror-calm water, native bush. Kayaking if you've got one.
Okarito Campground DOC Coastal, next to Okarito Lagoon — NZ's largest unmodified wetland. White herons, good kayaking.
4 Rainforest Retreat, Franz Josef Park The classic Franz base. Surrounded by rainforest, hot tubs on site. Walk to the village.
Franz Josef TOP 10 Park Reliable chain, mountain views, good for families. Close to glacier walks.
5 Lake Paringa DOC Forest lake with mountain backdrop. Fishing, swimming, quiet. Halfway between Fox Glacier and Haast.
Pleasant Flat DOC Wide valley floor near Haast Pass. Big mountain views up the valley. Peaceful, excellent stars.
6 Cameron Flat DOC Open grassy flats near Makarora. Mountain views up the valley. Good stop after Haast Pass.
Boundary Creek, Lake Wanaka DOC Right on the shores of Lake Wanaka. That view across the lake to the mountains. Unreal at sunrise.
Kidds Bush, Lake Hawea DOC Lake Hawea's best-kept secret. Native bush, lakefront, mountain views. Tiny — get there early.
7–8 Glendhu Bay, Wanaka DOC Iconic Wanaka campsite. Direct lakefront with views to Mt Aspiring. 10 min drive to town.
Wanaka TOP 10 Park Best facilities in Wanaka. Lakeside location, walking distance to town. Good with kids.
9–10 Moke Lake DOC Quiet lake 15 min from Queenstown. Mountain-reflecting water, no crowds. Best sunset spot near QT.
12 Mile Delta, Lake Wakatipu DOC Lakefront on Wakatipu, 10 min from Queenstown. Film location (LOTR: Ithilien). Busy in summer.
Queenstown Lakeview Holiday Park Park Walking distance to town. Lake and mountain views. Best facility option if you want town access.
11 Te Anau Lakeview Holiday Park Park Right on Lake Te Anau. Mountain panorama across the water. Walk to the glowworm caves jetty.
Cascade Creek DOC On the Milford Road, 45 min north of Te Anau. Forest and river setting. Good if you want a head start to Milford.
12 Milford Sound Lodge Park The only option right at Milford Sound. Premium. Book months ahead. Riverside rainforest setting.
13 White Horse Hill, Aoraki/Mt Cook DOC The best campsite in NZ. At the base of Aoraki. Wake up to NZ's highest peak. Hooker Valley Track starts here.
Glentanner Holiday Park Park On Lake Pukaki's edge. Full Aoraki view across turquoise water. 15 min from Mt Cook Village.
14 Lake Tekapo Holiday Park Park Lakefront with that famous turquoise water. Hot springs 5 min walk. Church of the Good Shepherd nearby.
